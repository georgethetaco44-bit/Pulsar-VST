# Mutate directions
One per audition slot, in this order. "## key: Label", then the text the model gets.

## darker: Darker
Darker and heavier: lower cutoffs and less fizz, more weight and drive in the low mids, slower or deeper movement. Never raise the sub's level.

## movement: Movement
More movement: tempo-synced motion with `beat` (1/4, 1/8 or 1/16 notes) on cutoff, fold, comb or formant amounts, or a rhythmic gate; the hero macro sets how much.

## brighter: Brighter
Brighter and sharper: more upper harmonics and bite (fold, saturation, comb, resonance), a faster attack and tighter envelopes; clean, no harsh fizz above 10 kHz.

## weirder: Weirder
Weirder: bring in one surprising technique from the reference (chaos, FM, formants, comb steps, resonators, bytebeat, crush) that changes the character, still playable and in tune.
